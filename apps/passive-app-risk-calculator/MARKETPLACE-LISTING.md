## Product title

Passive App Risk Calculator - Bandwidth and Rewards App Review Tool

## Short description

Estimate electricity, data, privacy, ISP, device, and payout-threshold risk before installing bandwidth-sharing or background rewards apps.

## Full description

Passive App Risk Calculator is a local browser tool for people who want to review background earning apps before installing anything on a device.

Enter the claimed monthly payout, payout threshold, device wattage, daily runtime, electricity cost, bandwidth use, data cap, overage cost, and risk flags. The app returns a monthly net estimate, cost breakdown, risk score, review checklist, and avoid/review/small-test recommendation.

Everything runs locally in the browser. There are no accounts, API keys, external calls, tracking scripts, uploads, or automatic installs.
