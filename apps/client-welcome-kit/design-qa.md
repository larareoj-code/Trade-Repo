# Design QA

The source package is a local-first utility for service-business onboarding. It uses a restrained operational interface: structured inputs on the left, generated packet and email outputs on the right, and print/export controls in the header and form.

## Evidence

- Desktop layout keeps the form and outputs visible together for repeated editing.
- Mobile layout collapses to one column and preserves full-width controls.
- Print stylesheet hides inputs and prints generated packet content cleanly.
- No third-party images, fonts, analytics, or APIs are required.

## Guardrails

- Draft-only: no autonomous sending or client outreach.
- No legal advice or contract replacement language.
- No earnings, client outcome, or approval guarantees.
- Project data stays in browser storage unless the user copies, prints, or exports it.

Result: ready for source publication and static hosting review.
