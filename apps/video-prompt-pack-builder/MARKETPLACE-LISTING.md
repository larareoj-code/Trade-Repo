## Product title

Video Prompt Pack Builder - No-API Video Planning Kit

## Short description

Turn one video idea into a structured prompt pack with hooks, scene plan, cover prompt, voiceover prompt, captions, negative prompts, continuity checks, and local exports. Runs entirely in the browser.

## Full description

Video Prompt Pack Builder helps creators, freelancers, and small product teams plan videos before spending money on generation, editing, or production.

Enter your idea, audience, outcome, format, tone, runtime, scene count, must-include notes, and avoid list. The tool instantly builds a practical planning pack: hooks, scene purposes, visual actions, camera guidance, prompts, negative prompts, production boundaries, and QA checks.

It is inspired by multi-stage video planning workflows, but it does not call any generation APIs. Everything runs locally in the browser, so there are no credits, accounts, subscriptions, tracking scripts, or surprise usage fees.

## Included

- Static HTML/CSS/JS app
- 15, 30, 45, and 60 second planning modes
- Vertical short, explainer, tutorial, and marketplace promo formats
- Hook directions and scene-by-scene shot plan
- Cover, voiceover, captions, production, scene, and negative prompts
- Continuity checklist and rights/safety QA checks
- Copy, TXT export, JSON export, and local browser save
- Original cover image, editable `cover.svg`, and app source files
