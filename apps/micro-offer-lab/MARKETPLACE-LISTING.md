# Micro Offer Lab - Marketplace Listing Draft

## Short Description

Turn one rough idea into a small digital product concept, bundle outline, listing draft, QA checklist, and launch notes.

## Full Description

Micro Offer Lab is a browser-based planning tool for creators, template sellers, freelancers, and solo operators who want to package repeatable know-how into a focused mini offer.

Paste a skill, audience, or rough idea. The tool returns a product title, buyer promise, deliverable list, storefront copy starter, launch checklist, and polish notes.
