# Micro Offer Lab

Micro Offer Lab is a static, local-first app that helps creators and solo operators turn one rough idea into a compact digital product offer.

It generates:

- a product title;
- buyer promise;
- bundle contents;
- storefront draft;
- launch checklist;
- polish notes.

The app is deterministic and runs in the browser. It does not require an account, API key, backend, payment connection, or user tracking.

## Publishing Notes

- Use original examples.
- Keep the product promise specific and easy to verify.
- Review marketplace copy before publishing.

## Suggested Product Use

Sell as a small creator toolkit or include it as a bonus inside a digital-product seller bundle.

Suggested price lane: `$9` starter or `$19` toolkit.
