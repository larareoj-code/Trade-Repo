# Sales Page Snack Pack

Sales Page Snack Pack is a single-file browser app that creates compact product page copy blocks for small digital offers.

It produces:

- headline and subhead;
- page section blocks;
- listing copy;
- FAQ text;
- launch checklist cues.

The app is deterministic, local-first, and does not require accounts, APIs, or analytics.

## Suggested Use

Sell as a small copy-planning tool, include it in a seller bundle, or use it as a lead magnet for digital product creators.

Suggested price lane: `$9` starter or bundle bonus.
