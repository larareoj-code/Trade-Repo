# Privacy Note

Shorts Hook Lab is designed to work entirely in the user's browser.

## Data handling

- Topics and generated kits are not sent to a server by this static MVP.
- Up to 20 recent kits are stored in the browser's `localStorage`.
- The Clear all action removes the saved history for this app in that browser.
- Text and JSON exports are created locally and downloaded by the browser.
- Printing uses the browser's standard print feature.

## Third parties

The included MVP has no analytics, advertising, API, payment, tracking, or third-party font requests.

If a seller adds hosting analytics, checkout, email capture, error reporting, or other services, this notice must be updated to identify those providers and their data practices.

## User responsibility

Users should avoid entering confidential client information, private personal data, or material they do not have permission to use. Generated output should be reviewed for accuracy and suitability before publication.
